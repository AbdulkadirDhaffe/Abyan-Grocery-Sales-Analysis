# Abyan Grocery Sales Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

A Python-based data analysis project exploring Abyan Grocery sales performance for 2024.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow, transforming raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and visualization.

**Workflow:**
Raw Data → Inspection → Data Cleaning → EDA → Visualization → Insights → Recommendations

## 🎯 Business Questions

This analysis focuses on questions such as:

* Which branch generates the highest sales?
* Which product category performs best?
* Which products generate the most sales?
* How do sales change over time?
* Which payment method is most preferred?
* How does sales performance vary across customer types and genders?

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Jupyter Notebook

## 🔄 Analysis Process

### 1. Data Inspection

Reviewed the raw dataset, structure, dimensions, data types, missing values, duplicates, and other data-quality issues.

### 2. Data Cleaning & Preparation

Handled duplicate records, standardized categorical values, addressed missing data, validated numeric fields, and prepared the dataset for analysis.

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis

Used descriptive statistics and aggregation techniques to examine sales performance across branches, categories, products, time periods, payment methods, and customer segments.

### 4. Data Visualization

Created business-focused visualizations to communicate important patterns and comparisons clearly.

### 5. Insights & Recommendations

Translated analytical findings into practical business insights and recommendations for management.

## 🔍 Key Findings

* Hodan-Taleh was the strongest-performing branch, contributing approximately 48.6% of total sales.
* Household was the highest-performing product category with approximately 8,122.74 in sales.
* Rice 5kg was the top-selling product, generating approximately 3,128.50.
* December recorded the highest monthly sales, while September recorded the lowest.
* EVC Plus was the most frequently used payment method, representing approximately 51.8% of orders.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Study the operational practices of the strongest-performing branch and apply relevant practices to lower-performing branches.
* Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing categories and products.
* Prepare inventory and promotions ahead of stronger sales periods.
* Continue supporting reliable digital payment options preferred by customers.
* Monitor lower-performing branches and categories to identify opportunities for improvement.
